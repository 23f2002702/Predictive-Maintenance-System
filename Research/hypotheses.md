# Research Questions and Hypotheses

## Overview

This project investigates the application of machine learning techniques to predictive maintenance in manufacturing environments. The study focuses on both the technical effectiveness of failure prediction models and the business value generated through proactive maintenance strategies.

---

# Research Questions

## RQ1: Technical Perspective

Can machine learning models accurately predict equipment failures using operational sensor data such as temperature, rotational speed, torque, and tool wear?

---

## RQ2: Business Perspective

Can predictive maintenance strategies reduce production downtime and maintenance costs compared to traditional reactive maintenance approaches?

---

# Hypotheses

## H1: Technical Hypothesis

Machine learning models can accurately predict machine failures using operational sensor measurements.

### Rationale

Operational sensor data contains patterns and indicators that can provide early warning signs of impending equipment failure. By learning these patterns, machine learning models can identify high-risk machines before breakdown occurs.

---

## H2: Business Hypothesis

Implementing a predictive maintenance strategy can reduce maintenance costs and production downtime compared to reactive maintenance approaches.

### Rationale

Early identification of potential failures enables proactive maintenance interventions, reducing unexpected breakdowns, improving equipment availability, and lowering operational costs.

---

# Expected Contributions

### Engineering Contribution

* Identification of key failure-related operating conditions.
* Development of a machine learning-based failure prediction framework.
* Improved understanding of machine health indicators.

### Business Contribution

* Quantification of potential downtime reduction.
* Estimation of maintenance cost savings.
* Demonstration of data-driven maintenance decision-making.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

1. The machine learning model achieves reliable failure prediction performance on unseen data.
2. High-risk machines can be identified before failure occurs.
3. Predictive maintenance scenarios demonstrate measurable reductions in downtime and maintenance costs.
4. The findings can be translated into actionable recommendations for maintenance planning and operational efficiency.
